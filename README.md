# Espee Kaipo — website

Static site for Espee Kaipo, Real Estate Consultant at Harcourts Te Atatu South, Auckland.

- Open `index.html` locally, or serve the folder (preferred, so fonts and video load cleanly):
  `python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory /workspace/website/espee-kaipo`
- Design follows the agency house style in `/workspace/website/style-guide-era.md`.
- Facts, sources, and gaps are in `FACTS.md`. Nothing on the page is invented: missing items are marked `[placeholders]`.
- The contact form posts to `[form endpoint]` and, until that is wired, tells the visitor to call or email instead.

Walkthrough videos (v2, compressed) live in `assets/video/`. Screenshots are in `screenshots/` after a capture pass.

Pages: `index.html` (home), `about.html`, `listings.html`.
