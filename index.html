About Espee

A neighbour
in the business

Espee Kaipo is a real estate consultant at Harcourts Te Atatu South, with Shelter Realty Ltd (licensed under the REAA 2008). She has lived and worked in West Auckland for more than 30 years, and brings that local knowledge to every conversation about buying or selling a home.

She is skilled in negotiation, sales and marketing, and makes a point of listening to what her clients actually need. She works in English and Filipino, and also in Spanish, Tagalog and Visayan, and is used to speaking with first-home buyers as well as investors, developers and mortgage brokers.

Buying and selling

What buyers asked for

West Auckland buyers often say the same things: they want a realistic sense of price before they spend time or money, they do not want to be pushed, they want honest information about condition, and if it is their first home they want the process explained in plain language. That is how Espee aims to work.

01

Price, said plainly

You should have a realistic sense of whether a property is in reach before you book a building report. If a home is for auction, Espee will be clear about what that means, and about what she can and cannot tell you in advance.

02

No pressure

Come to an open home, look around, and ask every question you have. You do not have to make an offer on the day. There is time to think, and there is usually time to come back for a second look.

03

Honest condition

Espee will share what she knows about a property’s condition and point you to the inspections and documents that still need doing. She will not promise that a house is free of defects.

04

If it is your first time

The process can feel opaque: open homes, due diligence, finance, auctions, and what happens after you have been through a property. Ask the “obvious” questions. They are not obvious if you have not done this before.

If you are selling

Espee will start by listening to what you need, then use her experience in negotiation, sales and marketing to plan the next step. Request an appraisal and she will be in touch.
