About Espee

The full picture

Real estate consultant at Harcourts Te Atatu South — local knowledge from more than 30 years living and working in West Auckland.

Biography

Who she is

Espee Kaipo is a real estate consultant at Harcourts Te Atatu South, part of Shelter Realty Ltd, which is licensed under the REAA 2008. The official job-title field on her Harcourts profile page is still published as “[position]”; “Real Estate Consultant” is the title used in her Harcourts biography and on her LinkedIn headline.

Her Harcourts biography describes an experienced consultant with a demonstrated history in the real estate industry, skilled in negotiation, sales and marketing, and equipped with the local knowledge that comes from living and working in West Auckland for more than 30 years. It also notes a habit of listening to her clients’ needs, and that she works with investors, developers and mortgage brokers as well as everyday buyers and sellers.

Her LinkedIn about text adds creative entrepreneurship, and that she studied overseas and at the University of Auckland.
